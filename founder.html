Why I am building this

The cafe I could not find.

I am Mahendra Pratap, an engineering student from Gorakhpur. I have been to many cafes. Not one of them was made for people who actually work.

The problems are always the same. The internet is slow. There is no charging point near your seat. The place is noisy. After an hour with a laptop, people start to stare. And if you want real food, you have to leave your work and come back.

So I decided to design the place I could not find. One space where fast internet, a charging point at every seat, a calm environment and good food come together. Start your day with chai, finish with dinner, and never leave your seat just to eat.

But a good place is more than good WiFi. I want engineers, founders, content creators and online workers to meet people who are building too. Share ideas, find partners, and grow faster together.

A place to focus, and people to grow with.

This is a plan today, not a finished cafe. I am sharing it early so you can help shape it.

The real problems

What goes wrong in most cafes.

Slow or unstable internet

Feeling watched after one hour with a laptop
No proper food, so you leave your work to eat

The plan

What I am building.

Six things that make this different. All planned, none invented.

Planned

Fast, steady internet

Dual-fiber WiFi, so video calls, uploads and deployments do not fail.

Planned

Charging at every seat

A socket and USB-C port at your table. No hunting for a plug.

Planned

A calm place to focus

A quiet, respectful space. No one stares and no one rushes you.

Planned

Food all day

Chai, coffee, snacks, breakfast and dinner. Eat without leaving your work.

Planned

Tables made for thinking

Whiteboard-top tables, so you can draw your ideas, not just type them.

Planned

People who build

Engineers, founders, creators and editors in one place. Meet, share and find partners.
